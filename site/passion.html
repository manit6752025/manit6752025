Why I build

I enjoy the space where technology meets curiosity: how systems behave, how networks can be improved, how AI tools can become more practical, and how open-source projects turn ambitious ideas into real software.

The goal isn’t to know everything. It’s to keep asking better questions, build things that teach me something, and leave the codebase a little better than I found it.
